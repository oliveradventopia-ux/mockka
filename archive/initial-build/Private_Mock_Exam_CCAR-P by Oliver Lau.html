Introduction & Certification Overview

  • What the Certification Exam is About: The Claude Certified Architect Professional (CCAR-P) certification validates technical expertise in designing, building, evaluating, and governing production-grade enterprise AI architectures using Anthropic's Claude platform. The exam assesses competence across 7 core domains: Solution Design & Architecture, Claude Models & Context Engineering, Enterprise Integration & Model Context Protocol (MCP), Evaluation & Optimization, Governance & Safety, Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle, and Developer Productivity.
  • Who the Certification is For: This professional credential is tailored for Enterprise Architects, Solution Architects, AI Systems Engineers, Technical Leads, and Technology Consultants responsible for leading end-to-end Claude deployment initiatives, evaluating pattern trade-offs, and establishing production guardrails for clients and partner networks.
  • Available Preparation Materials:
    • CCAR-P Architecture Recap & Reference Manual: Detailed breakdown of the 24 core architectural rules, pattern decision trees, and integration standards.
    • Full 63-Question Practice Mock Exam: Scenario-based questions covering single-choice, multi-select, and scenario-matching formats with explanations.
    • Interactive Performance Dashboard: Real-time domain scoring against the 75% passing threshold, mistake categorization, and targeted study plans.
  • Official Candidate & Partner Resources: Review official scheduling policies, certification guidelines, and exam FAQs on the Anthropic Skilljar Certification Portal (https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications).
