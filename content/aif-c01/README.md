# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) — Mockka practice exam

A 50-question, blueprint-weighted practice exam drawn from a 67-item originally-authored
bank, built with the [Mockka exam-compiler methodology](../../methodology/00-pipeline.md).

## Provenance and independence

These questions are **originally authored**. They were written against:

- the **official AIF-C01 exam guide, version 1.1** — the 5 domains, their percentage
  weights (20 / 24 / 28 / 14 / 14), the 14 task statements and 69 objectives, which
  supplied the concepts and the canonical vocabulary used throughout
- the **publicly published exam blueprint facts** — 65 questions (50 scored), 90 minutes,
  scaled 100–1,000 scoring with a 700 pass mark
- freely-published practice material analyzed under a strict clean-room rule (analytical
  classification only — concept and distractor-pattern per item, never text)
- general AI/ML and AWS platform knowledge

The full concept derivation is in [`derivation/`](derivation/), including which concepts
each source contributes, where they converge, and the merge decisions behind the 67-concept
inventory.

### Prior art

Two freely-published practice sets informed the concept derivation and distractor
calibration, credited here: **Darcy DeClute's AIF-C01 practice set** (TheServerSide) and
**Jason Walsh's MIT-licensed AIF-C01 question repository** (GitHub) — both analyzed as
classification-only sources; their derivation records are
[`derivation/source-declute-serverside.md`](derivation/source-declute-serverside.md) and
[`derivation/source-jwalsh-aif.md`](derivation/source-jwalsh-aif.md). Several other
widely-circulated banks were **rejected during source screening** because their content
corresponds to recalled exam material — the exclusions table with evidence is in
[`derivation/sources.md`](derivation/sources.md).

The questions, concepts and reasoning in this exam are its own. Every scenario, option,
distractor and rationale is originally written.

The real AIF-C01 exam includes ordering and matching item types beyond this exam's three
formats; matching is approximated as scenario-matching and ordering concepts are tested as
single-choice over candidate sequences — the exam intro page carries the full disclosure
