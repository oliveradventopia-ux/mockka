## Headline

**The bank's content is strong and its keys are right — and it cannot ship in this
form.** Two bank-wide surface defects make the exam solvable by test-wiseness alone:

1. **BD-1 · key-position-constant.** The key is the first option in 67/67 items
   (all 57 SC keys are `A`; all 7 MR keys are exactly `A,B`; all 3 SM items map
   scenarios to options in listed order). The player renders options in JSON order
   with no shuffle (`apps/web/components/question-card.tsx`, `Object.entries`), so
   this is the on-screen order. One attentive sitting discovers it within a handful
   of questions. The `distractor_patterns` maps also tag only non-key letters,
   leaking the key to any metadata-displaying tool.
2. **BD-2 · answer-surface-cue.** The keyed option is the longest in 51/57 SC items
   (median 134% of the longest distractor; 15 items ≥150%, max 214% at 5.04) and is
   stylistically marked — routinely the only option carrying a "since / rather
   than" justification rider or a multi-part enumeration. "Pick the longest, most
   hedged option" scores ~100% with zero AWS knowledge. The S2 distillation
   explicitly warned about this cue class (`source-declute-serverside.md`:
   "authoring must keep option shapes parallel within an item"); the warning did
   not survive into S3.

Both defects are mechanically fixable without touching what the items test. Full
records with required fixes and ratchet proposals (two candidate validator checks):
`eval/judge-scores.json` → `bank_defects`.
